# References and Reading Scope

The identifiers below match the sources used in this edition’s chapters. They preserve the manuscript’s reference keys, not a ranking of importance or reading depth. The chapters explain the specific claims and limitations; this list provides bibliographic information, the material actually consulted, and locations within it. The reading scope comes from the Chinese manuscript’s source records. Translating and typesetting this edition did not extend it into a new review of the original literature.

Research results cannot diagnose fictional characters. Method summaries, authors’ proposals, and this book’s interpretations are not interchangeable. Historical sources not cited in the current chapters are excluded from this list.
