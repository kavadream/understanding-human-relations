# English reader edition in preparation

The English edition will preserve the Chinese book’s arguments, fictional story facts, research limitations, and cultural settings. It will use natural English rather than literal sentence-by-sentence syntax.

Current materials: working chapter titles in `titles.json` and a working terminology guide in `glossary.json`. Full chapter translations are not yet included in this release. Human target-language review has not been performed.

Stable chapter IDs identify the source; `display_order` controls reading order. In particular, `chapter-16` and `chapter-17` appear as reader chapters 7 and 8. Translation drafts must record their exact Chinese source version and content hash.

Suggested changes are welcome through the project’s Issues and pull requests. Please distinguish translation corrections from proposed changes to the underlying argument. No English text should be described as accepted before an actual human target-language review is recorded.
