# English translation preview

All seventeen chapters are provided as a complete AI-assisted translation draft, together with the preface, copyright notice, and references. This is a preview pending human target-language review, not a final approved translation. The Chinese edition remains the source text.

My native language is Chinese. I welcome corrections and suggestions from native English speakers to help make this English edition clearer and more natural while preserving its meaning. — KK

Read `book.md` for the combined manuscript or use the chapter files listed in `book.json`. Downloadable preview files are in `dist/en/` and the repository’s Releases. Build instructions are in `tools/BUILD_EN.md`.

Stable IDs preserve the source identity independently of display order: chapter-16 and chapter-17 are reader chapters 7 and 8. `edition.json` records the exact cleaned Chinese chapter hashes, English draft hashes, source edition, and review status. `titles.json` and `glossary.json` document working English choices.

The translation preserves fictional story facts and settings, as well as distinctions between research findings, author proposals, the book’s interpretations, and value choices. Translation did not independently recheck the cited original literature or establish learning effectiveness.

For corrections, specify the chapter, edition, passage, and proposed wording. Explain whether the issue concerns translation accuracy, natural English, cultural clarity, or the underlying argument. Changes are subject to maintainer review; see the project’s content license. No human English-language acceptance is claimed.
